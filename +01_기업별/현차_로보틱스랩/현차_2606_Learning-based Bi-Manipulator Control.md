# Learning-based Bi-Manipulator Control

- Until hiring 2026-06-01 09:00 ~ Until hiring

## Who we are
The Robotics Lab brings together a diverse group of experts from various fields for the development of products based on robotics technologies. The Robotics Lab aims to develop technologies that provide a wide range of services in spaces where robots can coexist and work together with humans.

Under the Robotics Lab, we are a team that focuses on the control and design of medical and industrial wearable robots, as well as articulated robots, and we carry out a wide range of tasks from early-stage research to mass production.


## Responsibilities
In this role, we are developing and researching reinforcement/imitation learning-based control for precise task execution by a bi-manipulator robot in unstructured tasks.

■ Learning-based Robot Control
　· Control and simulation for a perceptive bi-manipulator
　· Developing behavior control and motion planning algorithms based on reinforcement learning/imitation learning

■ Control Programming
　· Programming real-time robot control algorithms
　· Programming simulation for machine learning
　· Optimizing algorithms for faster computation

■ System Programming
　· Developing system-level control and wired/wireless communication systems
　· Understanding and implementing CANopen and EtherCAT communication system

## Minimum qualifications
· Master's degree in mechanical engineering/electrical/electronic engineering/robotics engineering
· Minimum 5 years of experience in following:
　- Learning-based manipulator control and motion planning
　- Sim-to-Real development and implementations for robotics hardware
· Proficiency in English (fluent in both written and oral business communication
· Proficiency in C/C++/Python/MATLAB

## Preferred qualifications
· Ph.D in mechanical engineering/electrical/electronic engineering/robotics engineering
· Those who have experience in robotics, reinforcement/imitation learning-based control
· Proficiency in Real-Time Linux, EtherCAT, Learning Framework, and Python or C++
